# Scientific Tone Guide

## Cautious language

- suggest.
- indicate.
- are consistent with.
- may reflect.
- were associated with.
- provide evidence for.
- could contribute to.

## Causal language to avoid

- prove.
- confirm.
- determine.
- drive.
- demonstrate mechanism.
- establish causality.

## Suggested verbs for observational studies

- associate.
- correspond.
- vary with.
- co-occur.
- align with.
- support.
- suggest.

## Sentence patterns for Results

- "Samples from [group] showed [pattern], with [statistical evidence] ([figure/table])."
- "[Variable] was associated with [response], although [uncertainty or limitation]."
- "The strongest difference was observed in [comparison], where [effect direction] was [effect size]."

## Sentence patterns for Discussion

- "These findings suggest that [interpretation], although [limitation]."
- "This pattern is consistent with [possible process], but additional evidence is needed to test [mechanism]."
- "The association between [A] and [B] may reflect [ecological context]."

## Sentence patterns for limitations

- "Because this study was observational, we cannot infer direct causality."
- "Host predictions should be interpreted cautiously because they were based on [evidence type]."
- "AMG candidates require further validation before functional roles can be confirmed."
