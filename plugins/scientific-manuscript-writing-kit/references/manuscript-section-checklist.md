# Manuscript Section Checklist

## Title checklist

- Is the title specific and accurate?
- Does it avoid overstating causality or novelty?
- Does it include the main system, data type, or study focus when useful?

## Abstract checklist

- Background is concise.
- Objective is explicit.
- Methods are specific enough to understand the study design.
- Results include main patterns and evidence without invented numbers.
- Conclusion follows from the Results.

## Introduction checklist

- Broad context is clear.
- Specific knowledge gap is explicit.
- Study system and rationale are justified.
- Objectives and hypotheses are testable.

## Methods checklist

- Study design and sampling are reproducible.
- Laboratory methods are described with enough detail.
- Bioinformatics includes software, versions, databases, parameters, and filtering thresholds.
- Statistical analysis includes model formulas or tests.
- Data and code availability are addressed where required.

## Results checklist

- Main patterns are described before details.
- Statistical evidence is reported with effect direction and uncertainty where available.
- Results avoid mechanism-heavy Discussion language.
- Figures and tables are cited in logical order.

## Discussion checklist

- Main findings are summarized without repeating all Results.
- Comparisons with previous studies are supported by citations.
- Mechanisms are cautious and evidence-based.
- Limitations are explicit.
- Implications do not exceed the data.

## Conclusion checklist

- Conclusion is concise.
- Claims match the evidence.
- No new results or unsupported mechanisms are introduced.
