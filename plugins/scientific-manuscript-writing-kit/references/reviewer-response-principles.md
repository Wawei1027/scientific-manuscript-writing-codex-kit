# Reviewer Response Principles

## Polite tone

- Thank reviewers for constructive feedback.
- Keep responses calm, specific, and evidence-based.
- Avoid defensive language.

## Point-by-point structure

- Quote or summarize each comment.
- State the response strategy.
- Describe the change made.
- Provide page and line information when available.
- Mark unresolved items clearly.

## How to disagree respectfully

- Acknowledge the concern.
- Explain the scientific reason.
- Clarify what was changed in the manuscript.
- Offer an alternative revision when possible.

## How to report new analyses

- State what analysis was added.
- State why it addresses the comment.
- Report results cautiously.
- Provide manuscript location and supplementary material location.

## How to mark changes by page/line

- Use exact page and line numbers when available.
- If page and line numbers are unavailable, provide section and paragraph.
- Do not claim a change was made without a location.

## How to avoid overpromising

- Do not promise analyses that cannot be completed.
- Do not claim validation that was not performed.
- Use clear status labels such as accepted, partially accepted, respectfully disagreed, or requires additional analysis.
