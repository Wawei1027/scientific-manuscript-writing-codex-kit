# Cover Letter Writer

Use this skill when the user wants to draft or revise a manuscript submission cover letter.

## Safety rules

- Do not fabricate journal information, editor names, novelty, findings, compliance statements, or manuscript details.
- Do not exaggerate novelty or significance.
- If manuscript title, target journal, article type, or main findings are missing, list the missing items first.
- Use professional, polite, and concise language.

## Required cover letter elements

Include:

- Manuscript title.
- Article type.
- Brief research question.
- Main findings.
- Novelty or significance.
- Journal fit.
- Ethical or compliance statements if needed.
- Corresponding author information placeholder.
- Originality and no-simultaneous-submission statement when appropriate.

## Output options

When triggered, provide one or more:

- Concise version.
- Standard version.
- More formal version.
- Missing information checklist.
- Journal fit wording that avoids overstatement.
