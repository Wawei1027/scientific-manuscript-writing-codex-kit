# Manuscript Structure Editor

Use this skill when the user wants to evaluate or rebuild the overall structure of a scientific manuscript, especially ecology, microbiome, metagenomics, or virome manuscripts.

## Safety rules

- Do not fabricate citations, results, p-values, experimental details, journal requirements, sample sizes, or manuscript content.
- Preserve scientific accuracy and mark missing information as `[missing]`.
- Keep observed evidence, statistical inference, and ecological interpretation separate.
- Do not place real unpublished manuscripts, private reviewer comments, application materials, or sensitive data into public repositories.

## Structure checks

Check the logical relationship among:

- Title.
- Abstract.
- Introduction.
- Methods.
- Results.
- Discussion.
- Conclusion.

Evaluate whether:

- The research question is clear.
- The knowledge gap is explicit.
- The hypotheses are testable.
- The Results support the Conclusion.
- The manuscript has a coherent story from problem to evidence to implication.

## Three-layer feedback

Provide feedback in three layers:

1. Major scientific issues: research question, novelty, design, evidence strength, reproducibility, statistics, interpretation, missing controls, or unsupported claims.
2. Structure and logic issues: section order, paragraph flow, result narrative, figure order, repeated ideas, misplaced interpretation, or weak transitions.
3. Paragraph and sentence-level issues: clarity, concision, terminology, cautious language, and scientific English.

## Microbiome and ecology manuscript cautions

For ecology, microbiome, metagenomics, and virome manuscripts, specifically check whether:

- The research question is too broad.
- Correlations are overstated as causation.
- Methods are reproducible enough for amplicon, shotgun, virome, and statistical workflows.
- Results merely repeat figures without explaining patterns.
- The Discussion mixes observed evidence, statistical inference, and ecological interpretation.
- AMG candidates, host predictions, taxonomic assignments, and functional annotations are overstated.

## Output format

When triggered, provide:

- A brief manuscript diagnosis.
- A section-by-section issue list.
- The three-layer feedback table.
- A revised outline or restructuring plan.
- Claim-evidence-risk notes for major conclusions.
- Missing information that must be supplied before stronger editing.
